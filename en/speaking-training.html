02 / Topics

What we can
work on together.

Examples of topics that can be included in a proposal. The selection and depth depend on the team’s objectives and experience.

  1. When a chatbot is not enough

    Distinguish an AI answer from an agent that plans steps and uses tools. Decide where each approach makes sense in a health service.

  2. From evidence to useful communication

    Follow a demonstration of reading a public recommendation and preparing a meeting brief or patient information, checking sources and limitations.

  3. From scattered notes to organised work

    Turn fictional notes into minutes, an action plan and an activity report. Identify responsibilities, deadlines and missing information.

  4. AI agents and automation

    Explore how an agent can support an administrative workflow: organise requests, track their status and draft responses for human review. Tools and examples are selected to suit the team’s context.

  5. Before you trust it: test and correct

    Try incomplete requests, look for errors and check what the agent did. Approve significant actions before messages are sent or records are changed.

  6. Choose a starting point for your team

    Define a first use case, quality criteria and data access boundaries. Discuss how it could fit the team’s workflows.

05 / Before you join

Frequently asked questions

Do I need programming experience?

Not for introductory training. The technical level and prerequisites are tailored to the team’s objectives and experience.

Can training be online or in person?

Yes. We choose the format together based on the objectives, location and preferred way of working. There is no single format for every proposal.

Do I need to pay for or install AI tools?

Tools and requirements are specified in the proposal. Any installation, licence or additional cost is explained before confirmation.

Will we use patient data?

No. Demonstrations use fictional information or public documents. Do not share clinical data or identifiable patient information in your questions.

What certification is associated with the training?

The trainer’s pedagogical certification issued by IEFP is separate from DGERT certification of a training provider. Any attendance statement is specified in the proposal; no training credits or DGERT certification are advertised.

How are pricing and group size determined?

The proposal considers the objectives, content, format, duration and participants’ experience. Group size is matched to the type of work involved.

How can I take part?

Request a quotation. You will receive availability, the programme and participation terms by email, including payment, invoicing and cancellation information, before you decide.
